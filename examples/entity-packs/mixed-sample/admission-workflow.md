# Admissions playbook

## Admission Workflow

The end-to-end procedure for admitting a patient to the facility.

- step-1: Verify identity and insurance eligibility
- step-2: Complete demographic + emergency contact intake
- step-3: Collect chief complaint and initial vitals
- step-4: Assign attending physician (requires MD sign-off)
- step-5: Generate admission orders and route to pharmacy

## Sepsis Score

Numeric severity score computed at every encounter to detect sepsis early.
Higher scores indicate greater risk. Used by the triage nurse and attending
physician to escalate care.
